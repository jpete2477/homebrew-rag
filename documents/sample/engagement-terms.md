# Engagement terms (sample)

## Recording consent

Discovery sessions are recorded only with the explicit consent of every
participant. Recordings are deleted within 30 days of the final readout.

## Confidentiality

Client materials are held in confidence for three years following the end of
the engagement. Working files are stored on systems controlled by the
consultant and are not shared with third parties without written approval.

## Payment

Invoices are issued at kickoff and at readout, net 15.
