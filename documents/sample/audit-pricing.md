# AI Opportunity Audit — pricing

The AI Opportunity Audit is a fixed-fee engagement priced at $7,500. The fee
covers discovery interviews, process mapping, the effort-vs-impact matrix, and
the final recommendations readout.

## Turnaround

Three weeks from kickoff to readout, assuming stakeholder interviews are
scheduled within the first five business days.

## What is not included

Implementation work is scoped separately after the readout. Software licences,
cloud spend, and third-party API costs are billed at cost.
